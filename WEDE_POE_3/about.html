Our story

About Toronto Cupcake

Founded in 2010 by Michelle Harrison, Toronto Cupcake quickly established itself as a pioneer in the Canadian dessert industry by mastering the boutique delivery model.

Our brand blends artisan baking with dependable service, creating premium cupcake experiences for celebrations, corporate gifting, and everyday indulgence.

We focus on delivering artisanal, high-quality cupcakes with unmatched reliability, ensuring every order arrives beautifully presented and on time. Ready to taste the difference? See our cupcakes or request a quote for your next event.

What drives us

Our mission, vision and audience

To deliver artisanal, high-quality cupcakes with unmatched reliability for each and every client experience.

To maintain brand equity as Toronto's premier gift delivery service and continue setting the standard for premium seasonal gifting.

We proudly serve local dessert enthusiasts, high-volume corporate event planners, and B2B clients seeking premium seasonal gifting solutions.
